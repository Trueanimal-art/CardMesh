# Mobile popup refactor prompts

## Unified portrait popup frame

Create a production-ready 2D Unity mobile UI asset: ONE empty portrait popup
window frame for a premium fantasy card game, matching the CardMesh Emerald
Vault UI references. Tall portrait proportion approximately 0.61
width-to-height, designed for a 1080x1920 mobile canvas. Deep dark emerald
inner panel with very subtle velvety/card-table texture, refined antique gold
filigree border of consistent thickness, small emerald jewel accents at
corners, restrained luxury, crisp readable silhouette at phone size. Include a
small elegant circular header-medallion mounting point centered on the top
border and a small circular close-button mounting point at the upper right, but
NO icons and NO text. No large side diamonds, no horizontal WebGL-style
protrusions, no oversized ornaments intruding into content, no baked labels, no
UI controls, no scenery. Keep the whole interior clear and usable for layout.
Isolated single asset on a perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF background for
chroma-key removal.

## Centered Play CTA

Create a production-ready 2D Unity mobile UI asset: ONE centered horizontal
PLAY call-to-action frame for the CardMesh premium fantasy card game, matching
the Emerald Vault navigation and reward/chest art. Wide balanced emerald
plaque, symmetrical antique-gold filigree border, luxurious but clean, crisp at
phone size. On the left, include ONE compact unified emblem/medallion showing
three equal-sized linked cards or three equal linked nodes; do not use a fanned
staircase, cards of decreasing sizes, a spade suit, or a generic triangle play
icon. Keep the label area completely empty for live Unity TMP text. No baked
words, letters, extra buttons, or scenery. Isolated single asset on a perfectly
flat solid magenta #FF00FF background for chroma-key removal.
