# Bottom navigation iteration 2

Mode: built-in `image_gen`, followed by local `#ff00ff` chroma-key removal.
Exact Russian labels are composited deterministically with Pillow.

The previously acceptable option C remains the baseline. All new variants keep
its Emerald Vault materials, wide readable tabs and linked-card identity, while
removing the visually detached PLAY plaque.

## D — Integrated Command Bridge

One unified two-tier silhouette. The wide PLAY chamber is physically connected
to the lower four-tab rail through gold shoulders and rises only slightly from
the same frame.

## E — Five-cell Reliquary

One compact horizontal rail containing five cells. The center PLAY cell is
wider and brighter but remains part of the same frame; there is no floating
button or tall shield.

## F — Inverted Command Deck

Four broad secondary tabs form the upper tier. A wide reinforced PLAY bar is
integrated into the lower tier, closest to the thumb and visually anchored to
the bottom of the panel.

## Shared constraints

- No spade or other card-suit symbol.
- CardMesh emblem: overlapping cards plus connection lines and round nodes.
- No baked text, numbers or badges in generated sources.
- Bold consistent pictograms, no thin placeholder line art.
- Deep emerald enamel, muted antique gold, restrained ivory.
- Original game UI, not copied from the competitor reference.
