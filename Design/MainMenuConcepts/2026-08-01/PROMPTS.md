# CardMesh MainMenu — voting concepts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-08-01.
The supplied Unity MainMenu screenshot was used as the layout and brand
reference. All prompts preserve the central CardMesh logo/background and
redesign only the top activities and bottom navigation.

## A — Royal Emerald Enamel

Premium fantasy solitaire UI made from deep emerald lacquer, aged gold
filigree and warm ivory pictograms. Two substantial top medallion-buttons
with integrated badge sockets. A continuous ornate navigation dock with four
gold-rimmed tabs and a central raised emerald-and-gold spade CTA. Avoid flat
line icons, cartoon plastic, bright yellow blocks and excessive bloom.

## B — Card Reliquary

Refined fantasy card-game interface made from parchment mini-card plaques,
dark emerald leather and antique-gold edges. Horizontal top relic cards,
vertical mini-card navigation tabs and a central fan-of-cards Play CTA.
Avoid casino styling, generic clipart, bulky gold circles and cream/yellow
Play buttons.

## C — Emerald Vault

Modern premium jewelry-box UI made from emerald-black glass/enamel, thin
champagne-gold inlay and warm ivory typography. Horizontal translucent top
activity cards, a restrained continuous dock, coherent two-tone icons and a
wide low-profile heraldic Play shield. Avoid giant gold gradients, excessive
bevels, floating badges and slot-machine styling.

## Production note

After a concept is selected, export surfaces, icons, badges, glow layers and
pressed/active states as separate transparent PNG assets. Keep all Russian
labels and live counters in Unity TMP rather than baking text into sprites.
